# Complex Enough Support

**Publisher:** Huan Min Wei<br>
**Email:** support@complexenough.com

## Support channels

- Use the public [GitHub issue tracker](https://github.com/complex-enough/complex-enough/issues) for non-sensitive bugs, compatibility reports, documentation questions, and feature requests.
- Email [support@complexenough.com](mailto:support@complexenough.com) for account-independent private matters, privacy requests, suspected data exposure, or when public reporting would disclose sensitive information.
- Follow `SECURITY.md` for vulnerability reports. Do not open a public issue containing a vulnerability, secret, or private data.

## Scope

Best-effort support covers installation and plugin discovery, incorrect activation or failure to activate, role proposal and confirmation behavior, public contract validation, deterministic packaging, and reproducible compatibility defects in supported versions.

Support does not include custom implementation, guaranteed compatibility with untested hosts, professional advice, recovery of third-party accounts or data, or issues controlled solely by a host provider.

## Response expectations

Support is provided on a best-effort basis. There is no guaranteed initial-response or resolution time. Reports with a minimal reproduction, exact version, host, expected behavior, and observed behavior are easier to investigate.

Support is available in English and Traditional Chinese. Do not send passwords, API keys, access tokens, or unrelated personal information.
