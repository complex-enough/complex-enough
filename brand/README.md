# Complex Enough brand assets

The mark represents three materially different perspectives converging around one clear, auditable center. It is intentionally distinct from OpenAI branding and from generic AI brain or chat-bubble imagery.

## Files

- `complex-enough-mark.svg`: primary mark on a warm-light tile.
- `complex-enough-mark-dark.svg`: mark for dark surfaces.
- `complex-enough-mark-monochrome.svg`: one-color-compatible mark.
- `complex-enough-lockup.svg`: horizontal mark, name, and tagline.

## Core palette

| Name | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ink | `#182633` | Primary text and structure |
| Coral | `#E76F51` | First perspective and action accent |
| Teal | `#2A7F78` | Second perspective and evidence accent |
| Warm white | `#F6F1E8` | Background and central clarity |
| Slate | `#53606B` | Supporting text |

Keep clear space around the mark equal to at least one quarter of its width. Do not rotate, recolor individual pieces outside this palette, place text inside the mark, or imply endorsement by OpenAI.
