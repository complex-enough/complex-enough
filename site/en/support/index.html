Help

Support

Publisher: Huan Min Wei
Email: support@complexenough.com
Commitment: Best effort; no guaranteed response or resolution time.

Support channels

  • Use the public GitHub issue tracker for non-sensitive bugs, compatibility reports, documentation questions, and feature requests.
  • Email support@complexenough.com for privacy requests, suspected data exposure, or when public reporting would disclose sensitive information.
  • Follow the repository's security policy for vulnerabilities. Never open a public issue containing a vulnerability, secret, or private data.

Scope

Best-effort support covers installation and plugin discovery, incorrect activation or failure to activate, role proposal and confirmation behavior, public contract validation, deterministic packaging, and reproducible compatibility defects in supported versions.

Support does not include custom implementation, guaranteed compatibility with untested hosts, professional advice, recovery of third-party accounts or data, or issues controlled solely by a host provider.

What to include

  • Exact plugin version or commit.
  • Host product and relevant environment details.
  • The smallest reproducible case.
  • Expected and observed behavior.
  • Whether private data or a secret may be involved—without including the secret itself.

Support is available in English and Traditional Chinese. Do not send passwords, API keys, access tokens, or unrelated personal information.
