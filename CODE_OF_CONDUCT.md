# Code of Conduct

This project welcomes constructive participation from people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

Contributors and maintainers are expected to:

- discuss ideas and evidence without personal attacks;
- give specific, actionable feedback;
- respect privacy, consent, attribution, and intellectual-property boundaries;
- avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, spam, and deliberate disruption;
- disclose conflicts of interest that materially affect a review or decision.

Maintainers may edit or remove abusive content and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect the community. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented privately, and avoid exposing reporters or affected people unnecessarily.

Report conduct concerns through the private contact described in `SECURITY.md`. Do not use a public issue when a report contains personal, sensitive, or safety-related information.
